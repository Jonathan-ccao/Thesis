\section{Full Experimental Data}
\label{app:exp-data}

This section reports every pure water permeability (PWP) and salt rejection measurement, grouped by campaign and element. Each condition used fresh coupons (Section~\ref{sec:experimental-design}). Coupons were tested in pairs in the two-cell rig (L = left cell, R = right cell). In Campaign~2, coupons are numbered in the order they were tested within each condition. In Campaign~1, each condition used one coupon pair, identified by cell (L, R). For the no-clean conditions, that pair was tested in three repeat runs.

Flux was calculated as $J_w = V/(A\,t)$ with $A = \qty{0.0139}{m^2}$, and PWP as $J_w/\Delta P$, using the transmembrane pressure recorded for each trial. Observed salt rejection is $R = (1-\bar{\kappa}_p/\kappa_f)\times 100$, where $\kappa_f$ is the mean feed-tank conductivity and $\bar{\kappa}_p$ the mean of three permeate readings.

In the coupon tables, the repeat readings on each coupon are first averaged to give a coupon mean. The within-coupon SD (SD$_\mathrm{w}$) is the sample SD of those repeat readings. The condition mean and sample SD (the Mean $\pm$ SD rows) are then taken across coupon means, with $n$ the number of coupons. Where only one value exists, the SD is not available and is shown as ---. All statistics are calculated from unrounded values, so they may differ in the last digit from statistics recalculated from the rounded values shown.
