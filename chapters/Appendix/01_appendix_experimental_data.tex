\chapter{Experimental data}
\label{app:data}

This appendix lists the measurements behind the results in Chapter~\ref{ch:results}. Table~\ref{tab:app-coupons} gives the mean for each coupon and each condition. Tables~\ref{tab:app-pwp-4238A}--\ref{tab:app-pwp-4297B} give every pure water permeability (PWP) trial, and Table~\ref{tab:app-rejection} gives every salt rejection test.

\paragraph{Calculations.} Flux is $J_w = V/(A\,t)$ with $A = \qty{0.0139}{m^2}$, and PWP is $J_w/\Delta P$. Salt rejection is $R = (1-\kappa_p/\kappa_f)\times 100$, where $\kappa_f$ is the mean of 3--4 feed-tank readings and $\kappa_p$ the mean of three permeate readings.

\paragraph{Statistics.} Readings on each coupon are averaged first. The condition mean $\pm$ sample SD is then taken across coupons, so $n$ is the number of coupons. Values are rounded for display only.

\paragraph{Excluded and omitted data.}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item 4238C extensive clean, C5 and C6: listed but excluded from the means.
  \item Campaign~1 4238C as received and commercial clean: not reported. Only the 4238C no clean + IP condition from Campaign~1 is used.
\end{itemize}
